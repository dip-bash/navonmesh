OpenAI has surpassed $25 billion in annualized revenue and is taking early steps toward a public stock offering as soon as late 2026, according to people familiar with the matter. The milestone marks a staggering pace of commercial growth for a company that had minimal revenue just three years ago, and reflects the rapid enterprise adoption of large language model APIs and consumer AI subscriptions.

Anthropic is not far behind, approaching $19 billion in annualized revenue — a figure that would make it one of the fastest-growing software companies in history. Together, the two firms are reshaping how enterprise software is built and sold, with AI model APIs increasingly acting as a foundational layer beneath everything from customer service platforms to coding tools.

The potential OpenAI IPO would be one of the most closely watched public market events in years, given the company's valuation trajectory and its central role in the current AI cycle. Investors and analysts are watching how OpenAI manages its unusual structure — balancing a capped-profit model, a nonprofit board, and the commercial ambitions needed to justify its scale.

Whether the listing materializes in 2026 will depend in part on market conditions and whether OpenAI can demonstrate a sustainable path to profitability at its current compute spending levels.
