---
title: "Ocean Acidification Threatens Marine Biodiversity at Accelerated Rate"
category: "ENVIRONMENT"
readTime: "5 min read"
---

New scientific studies reveal that ocean acidification is progressing faster than previously estimated. The changing pH levels are disrupting marine ecosystems and threatening food security for millions of people dependent on ocean resources. Researchers call for immediate action to reduce carbon emissions and support marine conservation initiatives before irreversible damage occurs.
