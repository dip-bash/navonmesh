AT&T has made its Connected Spaces solution available on the AWS Marketplace, marking a significant step toward streamlining enterprise IoT deployments by bundling connectivity, edge intelligence, and managed services into a single purchasable offering. The listing allows businesses to provision and activate smart space capabilities — covering environmental monitoring, occupancy analytics, access control integration, and asset tracking — directly through their existing AWS accounts.

The move reflects a broader industry trend away from fragmented IoT deployments, where enterprises previously had to assemble connectivity, hardware, cloud infrastructure, and analytics from separate vendors. Unified platforms that abstract that complexity are increasingly preferred, particularly as enterprises scale IoT from pilot projects to building-wide or campus-wide rollouts.

For AT&T, the AWS Marketplace listing opens access to a massive base of existing enterprise cloud customers without requiring a separate procurement process. For AWS, the partnership adds a tier of managed cellular and edge connectivity that complements its own IoT services including AWS IoT Core and Greengrass.

Industry analysts see the listing as part of a wave of telco-cloud partnerships that will reshape the enterprise IoT market in 2026 — with telecoms contributing connectivity and field infrastructure while hyperscalers provide the compute, storage, and AI analysis layer that turns sensor data into actionable intelligence.
